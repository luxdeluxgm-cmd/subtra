About

          
        


        

          

            Subtra is a daily subtractive color-mixing puzzle created by Jan Tokarzewski.
          

          

            Unlike additive screen colors (RGB), print pigments work by subtracting light from paper using Cyan, Magenta, Yellow, and Key (Black). Subtra challenges you to eyeball and mix the daily swatch in five tries.
